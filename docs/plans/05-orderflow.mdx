# Orderflow

**Which Gexbot plan this is.** Orderflow — classified leftover as a time series.

**What you will be able to do.** Name a print with the 2×2, tell spike from sequence from noise, and treat the bar as the moment pressure was created.

**What you already need.** [State](/docs/plans/04-state) DEX and convexity.

## Running example

**Bought 500 ATM calls at 10:05.** +DEX, +convexity, +GEX OF → 2×2 names **long call**. Not “bullish”: paid convexity with a dealer who chases.

**Sold 500 ATM calls.** −DEX, −convexity, **+GEX OF** → **short call**. Same +GEX OF bar, opposite cell. That is why GEX OF is not a buy signal.

## Summary

Orderflow is the increment of the leftover you already learned to locate. Name the 2×2 or do not name the print.

## Newcomer questions

1. **+GEX OF means buy?** No. It means call-sided leftover grew. On SPX that is often selling (*folklore*).
2. **One spike is the day-type?** No. Sequence names the day; a spike marks a level.
3. **Can I use the 15:50 net on a 09:35 fill?** Labeling leak.
