# What Gexbot is

**Which Gexbot plan this is.** The map of all plans.

**What you will be able to do.** Name Classic, State, Orderflow, Quant, and Research, and say what pressure each can name.

**What you already need.** [How to use this book](/docs/intro).

| Plan | What it can see | Pressure it can name |
|---|---|---|
| **Classic** | Unsigned GEX from OI and from volume | **Unknown.** Location is real; owner is not. |
| **State** | Classified residual ladders (OP, GEX profile, DEX, convexity) | Customer **incentive** and dealer **mandate** on the leftover |
| **Orderflow** | Time series of that leftover (`dexoflow`, `gexoflow`, `cvroflow`) | Mandate at the **print** (increment); day’s lean in the nets |
| **Quant** | API / WebSocket, cash RTH 09:30–16:00 ET | Same objects as State/OF, live. Explicit-expiry groups are live-only. Not a trading chapter. |
| **Research (gbR)** | Any-ticker surfaces | Sibling product. Not the classified engine. Not this course. |

The **classification engine** is the capability that turns Classic into State and Orderflow. Chapter 3 teaches it, and teaches the hedge chain once.

NQ/ES on Gexbot is a **multiplier conversion** (`ES_SPX`, `NQ_NDX`), not CME volume. One paragraph here; [Chapter 6](/docs/layer/06-nq-es-layer) is the full claim.

## What no plan shows

The hedge itself. How much is already hedged. Overnight inventory beyond Classic OI. Whether the customer hedged their own delta. Open vs close. A futures print you can point at and call “the dealer.”

## Summary

Sit Classic first (unsigned map). Then the engine that upgrades it. Then State and Orderflow. Then where the hedge lands (ES/NQ) and when the clock family changes.

## Newcomer questions

1. **Is Classic “the GEX map” people tweet?** It is the closest Gexbot cousin. Ownership is still unknown.
2. **Does State tell me who will buy ES next?** It names leftover paper. The initial delta hedge is mostly already done. Pending flow lives on gamma and on the clock.
3. **Can I skip to Orderflow?** Not if you cannot say why Classic ≠ State.
