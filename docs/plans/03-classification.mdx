## The hedge chain

<GexbotConcept id="hedge-chain" />
<WhoMustAct id="hedge-chain" />

<HedgeChain />

Liquidity roles (*inferred*, never observed on the futures tape):

- Dealer **long** gamma hedges against the move → supplies liquidity → pin / calm.
- Dealer **short** gamma hedges with the move → takes liquidity → amplify / trend.
- Do not invert that polarity.

Dealers hedge in **bands** (*market-general*). A later ladder gives direction and location, not the size or the second of the hedge.

A customer may **self-hedge** (buy calls and sell futures). Two hedgers ≈ no net forced flow in the underlying. Gexbot cannot see this. DEX implicitly assumes an unhedged directional customer (*inferred*).

## Running example — both branches

10:05 ET. 500 SPX 0DTE ATM calls, Δ 0.50. Share-equivalent: 500 × 0.50 × $100 = $25,000 per point ≈ **500 ES** ($50/pt). *Market-general* multipliers.

**Bought branch.** Dealer is short 500 calls, short gamma. Initial hedge: buys ≈ 500 ES now — **done** by the time a DEX bar exists. Spot +15, Δ → ~0.80 (SoT toy) → dealer buys ≈ 300 more ES **into strength** (gamma re-hedge, pending, path-conditional).

**Sold branch.** Dealer is long those calls, long gamma. Sells ≈ 500 ES now. Into the same rip, sells ≈ 300 more (fades).

**Same Classic bar, opposite forced re-hedge.** That sentence is why Classic ≠ State.

## Forced-flow sentence (gate)

> Actor · pressure · trigger · direction · venue — or “unknown.”

Classic wall: **unknown**.  
Bought-branch State long-call leftover: **dealer · mandate · spot up · buy ES (chase) · ES** (pending gamma) and **customer · incentive · approach · may dump (*folklore*)**.

## Summary

Classification signs leftover paper. The hedge chain maps each link to a screen and to what that screen cannot show. “Forced” is only mandate.

## Newcomer questions

1. **Did they just put on longs?** Not stated. Buy to close looks the same.
2. **Is the dealer about to buy because DEX printed?** The initial hedge is mostly already done. Pending force lives on gamma and on the clock.
3. **Quiet ladder means a dead day?** Residual can be tiny while two-way volume is huge. Pressure: none.
