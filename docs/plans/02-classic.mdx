# Classic

**Which Gexbot plan this is.** Classic — unsigned GEX from open interest and from volume.

**What you will be able to do.** Read the histogram (right call / left put), name zero gamma and majors, and write WhoMustAct as **unknown**.

**What you already need.** [What Gexbot is](/docs/plans/01-what-gexbot-is).

## Call vs put netting

<GexbotConcept id="call-put-netting" />

## Running example (10:05 ET)

A customer trades **500 SPX 0DTE ATM calls**. Classic’s volume-GEX bar at that strike grows **to the right** (call side).

Now the mirror: the customer **sold** the same 500 calls. Classic’s bar still grows **to the right**. **The bar is identical in the sold branch.** Owner unknown. Pressure unknown.

That is why Classic is not State. Chapter 3 splits the branches.

## Newcomer question

**Is a big green bar bullish?** It is call-sided gamma. On SPX it is often sold calls (*folklore*). Whether it stalls or fuels depends on owner and vol regime, and Classic cannot show the owner.

## Summary

Classic is the unsigned cousin of public GEX maps. Every WhoMustAct row on this page says **unknown**. Location can be real while sign is not.
