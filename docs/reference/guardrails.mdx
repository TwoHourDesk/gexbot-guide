# Professional guardrails

Do not oversimplify. Every chapter applies the rows that touch its screen. Facts marked **market-general** are not Gexbot claims.

1. **Hedge in bands.** Dealers hedge at their own thresholds, not tick by tick (*market-general*; standard market-maker risk practice). A ladder gives direction and location of pressure, not timing or size.

2. **Dealer is a population.** Shorthand for delta-neutral market makers, not one desk. Populations disagree and net internally.

3. **Self-hedged customers.** The counterparty may be another customer (cancels) or a customer who hedges their own delta (a vol fund buys calls and sells futures). Two hedgers ≈ no net forced flow. Gexbot cannot see this. DEX implicitly assumes an unhedged directional customer. *Inferred*.

4. **Residual is today so far.** Classic OI inventory already has its hedge on; only the re-hedge on movement is pending. State ladders start empty at 09:30.

5. **Open vs close is unknown.** A +DEX bar can be a new long or a short covering. Hedge inference can survive; intent inference cannot.

6. **Walls are hypotheses.** Tested by acceptance through the node. A concentrated −convexity node is a magnet or a trapdoor depending on vol regime and clock.

7. **Gamma re-hedge is path-dependent.** Forced flow ahead depends on where spot goes. The screen cannot know that.

8. **Vol regime governs the customer channel.** Do not apply the falling-vol wall/fuel map on a rising-vol tape.

9. **Converted NQ/ES.** The print is leftover NDX (or SPX) options. The inferred hedge, if it happens, is most likely to land in NQ (or ES) because that is the practical door for large index delta. NDX is thinner than SPX; treat NQ reads as weaker evidence. QQQ leftover is a different pile. *Inferred*.

10. **Toy numbers.** Order-of-magnitude only. The only numeric thresholds in the book are Gexbot’s $800MM / $1000MM −vanna notes, labeled folklore.

11. **Never name a futures print “the dealer hedge.”** You cannot identify it on the tape.

12. **Settlement.** NDX and SPX options are cash-settled European; QQQ and SPY options are physically settled American (*market-general*; exchange contract specifications). Do not transfer 0DTE resolution across that cut without saying so.

13. **Overnight structure has no leftover.** A high or low printed while cash was closed has no State residual at 09:30. Only Classic open interest can mark the location, and its pressure word is **unknown**.

14. **Futures-tape delta is not DEX.** Who is lifting or hitting the ES or NQ book is a different object from leftover share-equivalent. Do not add them or read one as confirming the other.

15. **Alerts time a touch; structure names how it arrived.** A ping is not a sweep, a break, or a rotation. The tape sentence goes in `Structure:`. Neither the ping nor the tape shape is the read by itself.

16. **Plain words for structure.** Swing high, range break, sweep, gap, VWAP. Do not import a methodology’s named levels.

Sources for market-general rows: Cboe SPX/SPY contract specs; CME ES ($50/pt) and NQ ($20/pt) contract units; standard options market-making (delta-neutral books, discrete hedge bands) as in Natenberg, *Option Volatility and Pricing*.
