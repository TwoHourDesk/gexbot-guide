## Settlement (*market-general*)

SPX options are European and cash-settled; daily expiries settle PM. SPY options are American and physically settled. Do not transfer SPX 0DTE resolution onto SPY without saying so. Gexbot shows none of the resolution link directly.

## Running example at 15:30

Spot has drifted away. The 500 calls are OTM. Charm drains their delta.

**Bought branch.** Customer is getting shorter (OTM long call dies). Dealer’s long-ES hedge is now too big → dealer **sells** (bar left, passive selling).

**Sold branch.** Mirror: dealer **buys** (bar right).

Flow **dies** if spot walks back to the strike and the calls go ATM (*docs*).

## Summary

Do not pool clock families. Last hour is not the open with a magnet pasted on. −vanna/charm are beta and residual-only.
